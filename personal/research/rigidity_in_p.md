---
title: Logarithmic prismatic $F$-gauges and rigidity of tame motives
author: [Cheni Yuki Yang](/yuki.md)
status: ongoing
---

We give two constructions of the category of logarithmic prismatic $F$-gauges. The first one arises in the spirit of [CF26](/bib/CF2026prismatic.md) and the second one is a stacky approach à la Bhatt-Drinfeld-Lurie. We also define the $\mathbb{A}^{1}$-localized version of them, thus provided a rigidity result for tame motives considered in [Mer25](/bib/merici2025tame.md). We discuss some connections with prismatic homotopy theory.
